# Archive-safe handoffs

`vl-handoff` now identifies the old owning chat and reports archive safety with a
reason and manual action. The saved handoff reconciles associated/attached trees,
workers and ownership, required exact state/local setup and successor access.
Unsafe or unknown preservation keeps the old owner unarchived. Codex teaching
links its managed/permanent lifecycle evidence and preserves other-host uncertainty.

Source, generated-copy and rendered teaching checks verify instruction delivery;
they do not establish a live archival transfer. No archival/cleanup authority changes.
