# Canon: brief prior findings are rebuttable by chip measurement

Any brief-supplied prior finding (certificate, exclusion list, baseline, "known"
constraint) is **rebuttable by the executing chip's own direct measurement**. On
contradiction the chip **STOPS** and raises it — never silently complies. Briefs
must never mark a prior finding binding against measurement. Context order taught
alongside: `direct measurement > dated ruling > record prose > recency/salience`.
